## 1. What this covers

This Return Policy applies to purchases made on https://onestarry.com. It sets out how to return an item for a refund or exchange, what condition we need it in, and how quickly you get your money back. It applies in addition to — and never instead of — your statutory consumer rights, and in addition to the 14-day right of withdrawal available to consumers in the EU, UK and Northern Ireland (https://onestarry.com/legal/withdrawal).

## 2. Your 30-day return window

You may return an item for a full refund of its price within 30 days. The window starts on the day you receive the item, or, for a single order delivered in several parcels, on the day you receive the last item. You do not need to give a reason.

## 3. Condition of returned goods

Returns are accepted where the item is unused, unwashed, unaltered and in the original packaging and condition you received it in, with any tags, accessories, manuals and protective film included. Please use the original box as a shipping outer where you can, or place our box inside a second one — we cannot refuse a return because an outer shipping carton was damaged, but a damaged product that was not protected in transit may be treated as diminished value (Section 7).

Inspecting an item to decide whether you want to keep it is fine, and is what the window is for. Using it as your normal item is not.

## 4. Items we cannot accept

- Goods that must be sealed for hygiene or health reasons and that have been unsealed after delivery (for example personal-care, food-contact, or intimate items where described as such on the product page).
- Customised or made-to-order goods, unless they are faulty or not as described.
- Gift cards and digital content once delivery has begun and you consented.
- Items sold as final sale, where that was stated clearly before you bought them.
- Goods that show damage or wear beyond what a normal inspection would cause, beyond the diminished-value adjustment in Section 6.

If your item is not eligible under this section, we will still handle a fault or a wrong delivery under Section 9 of our Terms of Service.

## 5. How to start a return

Email support@onestarry.com with your order number (it looks like SO-XXXXX-XXX, printed on your order confirmation) and the item or items you want to return. We will reply with a return authorisation and the address to send the parcel to. Please do not ship anything back before that authorisation: an unauthorised parcel may sit unprocessed, and we cannot refund what we cannot match to an order.

You have 14 days from the date we authorise the return to hand the parcel to the carrier.

## 6. Who pays the shipping

For a change of mind, you pay the cost of sending the item back to us. Use a service with tracking, or insure the parcel if the item is worth more than the basic carrier liability, because the item is your responsibility until we receive it.

We pay the return shipping — and the re-delivery shipping — where the item is defective, damaged in transit, incorrectly described, or is not the item you ordered, and in those cases we will also refund the original shipping you paid for that order.

If a return contains several items and only some are faulty, we refund the shipping proportionally to the faulty part of the order.

## 7. Refunds

We process your refund within 5 business days of receiving the returned parcel, or of receiving proof that you sent it, whichever is earlier and reasonable in the circumstances. The refund is issued to the original payment method; we do not pay refunds in cash or to a different card unless the original method is no longer available, in which case we will contact you.

Once we have issued it, your bank or card issuer normally shows the credit within 5 to 10 business days, sometimes longer for international cards. That timing is theirs, not ours. If more than 15 business days have passed since we confirmed the refund, contact support@onestarry.com with your order number and we will trace it.

Refunds cover the price of the returned items and, where the whole order is returned or the return is due to a fault of ours, the original shipping. Any diminished value under Section 3 is deducted from the item price and we will tell you the amount before we finalise the refund, so you can ask us to send the item back instead.

## 8. Exchanges

We do not operate an automatic exchange. Return the item under Sections 2 to 7 and place a new order for the size, colour or model you want. If the item is in stock, the new order is handled like any other order.

## 9. Damaged, defective or wrong items

Tell us within 30 days of delivery, quoting your order number, and attach a photograph of the item and of the packaging as it arrived. We will arrange a prepaid return label, then replace the item or refund it, at your choice, including the shipping you paid. Where a replacement is impossible, the refund is full.

## 10. Cancellations before dispatch

You can cancel an order at any time before it is packed for shipment and receive a full refund, including shipping. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number; if the parcel has already left our warehouse, use the return route above instead.

## 11. Your legal rights

Nothing in this Policy affects your statutory rights — including rights in respect of goods that are faulty, not as described, or do not match a sample — or, for consumers in the EU, UK and Northern Ireland, the 14-day right of withdrawal described at https://onestarry.com/legal/withdrawal. Where this Policy is more generous than the law, we will follow this Policy.
