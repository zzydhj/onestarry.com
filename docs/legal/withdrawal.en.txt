## 1. Who this notice is for

This notice applies only if you are a consumer concluding a contract for purposes outside your trade, business or profession, and you are habitually resident in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or Northern Ireland. It sets out your statutory right of withdrawal. It is information about a legal right, not a separate commercial promise, and it prevails over any conflicting term in our Return Policy or Terms of Service. If you are outside these regions, see our Return Policy at https://onestarry.com/legal/returns, which we apply as a matter of goodwill on similar terms.

## 2. Your right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period expires 14 days from the day on which you, or a third party other than the carrier and indicated by you, acquires physical possession of the last item ordered. Where an order is delivered in separate lots or pieces, the period runs from the day you receive the last lot or piece.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must send us a clear statement of your decision (for example a letter sent by post, or an email) before the withdrawal period expires.

Address: OneStarry LLC, [REPLACE: postal address, country]. Email: support@onestarry.com.

The deadline is met if you send the communication exercising your right of withdrawal before the period has expired. You may use the model withdrawal form in Section 6 below, but it is not mandatory.

## 3. Consequences of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we must refund to you all payments we received from you, including the delivery costs (with the exception of additional costs arising from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive standard type of delivery offered by us).

We will make that refund without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we receive the communication informing you of your withdrawal from this contract. We may withhold the refund until we have received the goods back, or until you have supplied evidence that you have returned the goods, whichever is the earliest.

We will make the refund using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event you will not incur any fees as a result of the refund. The date on which the credit appears on your account depends on your bank or card issuer.

## 4. Cost of returning the goods

You must bear the direct cost of returning the goods if you withdraw from this contract. You are liable only for any diminished value of the goods resulting from the handling of the goods other than what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods. In other words, you may inspect and handle the goods as you would reasonably be allowed to in a shop, but not use them.

Where an item cannot normally be returned by post because of its nature, we will collect it, and we will tell you the estimated collection cost before you withdraw.

## 5. Exceptions

The right of withdrawal does not apply, in particular, to contracts:

- for the supply of goods made to your specifications or clearly personalised;
- for sealed goods which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons and which were unsealed after delivery;
- for the supply of audio or video recordings or computer software which were unsealed by you after delivery;
- for the supply of digital content which is not supplied on a tangible medium, if the performance has begun with your prior express consent and your acknowledgement that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal;
- for the supply of perishable goods or goods which are firmly sealed to other items and cannot be separated.

Your statutory rights in respect of faulty or mis-described goods are not affected by this section.
